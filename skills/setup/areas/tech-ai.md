# Area block: information technology, artificial intelligence and digital regulation

Loaded by `/legal-kit:setup`. Two question groups, two minutes.

## Q1: Which instruments

> Which of these actually reach your work?
>
> - The Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)
> - The Digital Services Act, the Digital Markets Act
> - The Data Act, the Data Governance Act
> - Network and information security (NIS2, and Act No. 264/2025 Coll.)
> - The Cyber Resilience Act, the Product Liability Directive
> - Electronic identification and trust services
> - Platform-to-business, consumer and unfair commercial practices

**Record** the list. Each is reachable by alias through `eu_celex_lookup`.

## Q2: Role and timing

> Two questions, and the second is the one that bites.
>
> - Under the Artificial Intelligence Act, are you a **provider**, a **deployer**,
>   an importer, a distributor, or advising someone who is? The obligations differ
>   sharply and the role is assessed per system, not per company.
> - Which obligations are already running for you, and which are still ahead?

On the second, say plainly that the dates have moved and must be checked rather
than remembered:

> The Artificial Intelligence Act has been amended, so the applicable dates are
> not the ones in the original text and not the ones most summaries give. I check
> the consolidated version with `eu_act_versions` before relying on any date,
> and I will do that rather than quote from memory.

Also ask:

> Has the Czech adaptation act been adopted? Last time this was checked it had
> not, and until it has a number in the Sbírka zákonů there is nothing
> authoritative to cite. If you know otherwise, tell me and I will verify it.
