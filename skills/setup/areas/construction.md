## Q2: Which building act applies

This is the question that most often goes wrong, so ask it directly.

> Czech building law changed over: Act No. 283/2021 Coll. replaced Act No.
> 183/2006 Coll., with transitional provisions that keep the old act alive for
> matters already under way.
>
> Do your live matters run under the new act, the old one, or both? I will resolve
> the applicable version with `cz_act_versions` for any specific question rather
> than assuming, and case law decided under the old act has to be read against the
> version it applied.

Also:

> Does the **jednotné environmentální stanovisko** feature in your permitting
> work? It consolidated a stack of separate environmental opinions from 2024 and
> changed which authority issues what.
