## Q2: What the sources cover, and what they do not

State the limit plainly rather than discovering it mid-answer.

> Criminal case law here means the **Nejvyšší soud** Tdo docket, which is fully
> searchable, and the **Ústavní soud**, which matters for fair-trial questions.
>
> What is not here: first-instance and appellate criminal judgments are largely
> outside the indexed open data, and there is no equivalent of the administrative
> court search for the criminal chain. For anything below the Supreme Court I can
> tell you what the indexed range contains, and no more than that.

Then:

> Does **corporate criminal liability** under Act No. 418/2011 Coll. feature in
> your work? It sits next to compliance programmes and is worth flagging when a
> commercial question touches it.
