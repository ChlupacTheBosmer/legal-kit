## Q2: The doctrines that do not exist here

Say this once, and record that it was said:

> Three things from Anglo-American practice have no Czech equivalent, and the
> `claude-for-legal` plugins will produce all three if left uncorrected:
>
> - **Fair use** does not exist. Czech law has an enumerated list of exceptions
>   and limitations in Act No. 121/2000 Coll.; if a use is not on the list it is
>   not excused by being fair.
> - **Assignment of copyright** is not possible. Moral rights are inalienable and
>   economic rights are licensed, not transferred. What a contract can do is grant
>   an exclusive licence, and the difference matters in drafting.
> - **Work made for hire** does not exist. The employee work regime
>   (zaměstnanecké dílo) in § 58 means the employer *exercises* the economic
>   rights; the employee remains the author.
>
> I correct these on sight and say what was corrected. `docs/jurisdiction-overlay.md`
> has the full list.

Then:

> Do you draft in English for foreign counterparties? That is where these get
> smuggled back in, because the English vocabulary carries the American concepts.
> I will flag "assigns all right, title and interest" and its relatives.
