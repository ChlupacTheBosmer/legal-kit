# Area block: civil obligations, procedure and enforcement

Loaded by `/legal-kit:setup`. Two question groups, two minutes.

## Q1: Substance or procedure

> Which is your work: substantive civil law (obligations, property, liability,
> family), civil procedure, or enforcement (výkon rozhodnutí, exekuce)?
>
> And do you litigate, or advise short of litigation?

---

## Q2: What the sources actually give you

Be honest about the shape of the case-law coverage, because it differs by court
and the difference decides what an answer can claim.

> Three levels, and they are not equally covered:
>
> - **Nejvyšší soud**: every published decision, searchable by text, headnote,
>   index keyword, case number or ECLI. Category **A** means published in the
>   official collection and carries the most weight; B to E descend.
> - **Ústavní soud**: every decision. A **nález** decides the merits and binds; a
>   **usnesení** is usually a rejection on admissibility and says little about
>   substance. I will not cite one as authority for a proposition.
> - **District and regional courts**: a local index built from Ministry of Justice
>   open data, covering only the date range that has been built, and holding the
>   civil and criminal dockets only.

Then:

> How far back do you need the first-instance material? The index builds at a few
> minutes per year of coverage, so this is a question about your patience, not a
> hard limit.

If litigating, add:

> Do you want the **prejudikatura** surfaced by default, meaning what a decision
> relied on and how it treated it? For the Supreme Administrative Court I have
> that structurally; for the others I have to read the decision.
