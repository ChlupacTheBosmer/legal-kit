# Area block: data protection and privacy

Loaded by `/legal-kit:setup`. Three question groups, two minutes.

## What it drives

Which side of a data processing agreement the user sits on, whether ÚOOÚ guidance
is searched before answering, and whether a data protection impact assessment is
presumptively required for their work.

---

## Q1: Controller or processor

> Two questions that decide almost everything downstream.
>
> - Are you mostly a **controller** (your own purposes, your own data subjects), a
>   **processor** (customers' data, their purposes), or both depending on the product?
> - Which regimes actually reach you? The General Data Protection Regulation
>   (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and Act No. 110/2019 Coll. are the baseline. Anything
>   sectoral: electronic communications, health, employment monitoring, financial?

**Record** as `**Role:**` and `**Regimes:**`.

---

## Q2: The regulator's position

> For a GDPR question the Czech data protection authority (Úřad pro ochranu
> osobních údajů, ÚOOÚ) has usually said something, and it is often more decisive
> in practice than the Regulation itself. legal-kit mirrors everything it has
> published since 2017.
>
> It is the supervisory authority's interpretation: persuasive, tells you how the
> regulator will approach a matter, **not binding law**, and a court can depart
> from it. I cite it as the regulator's position with its date, never as the rule,
> and always paired with the provision it construes.

If the ÚOOÚ index is not built, offer it now: it takes seconds.

> Do you have recurring questions the regulator has addressed? Camera systems,
> direct marketing, employee monitoring, cookies? I will note them so I check the
> guidance before answering rather than after.

---

## Q3: Impact assessments

> Two things.
>
> - What triggers a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) in your practice?
>   ÚOOÚ maintains the Article 35(4) list of operations that always require one.
> - Do you have a house DPIA or privacy impact assessment template? Paste it or
>   give me a path and I will match its structure rather than inventing one.

**If the user's work involves artificial intelligence or an entirely novel
processing solution,** note that both appear on ÚOOÚ's Article 35(4) list as
criteria, which makes a DPIA presumptively mandatory rather than discretionary.
Say it once, and record it.
