# Contracts

Drafts and reviews. **Gitignored below this README**: what you put here is yours,
and frequently a client's.

| Naming | Example |
| --- | --- |
| `YYYY-MM-DD-counterparty-type.md` | `2026-09-13-acme-nda.md` |
| `YYYY-MM-DD-template-<name>.md` | `2026-09-13-template-smlouva-o-dilo.md` |

Czech contracts are drafted in Czech, in full, not translated from an English
draft. Summarise the result in English so it can be checked without re-reading
the Czech. See `CLAUDE.md` under "Language".

Every provision cited in a draft goes through `legal_cite` first, and is written
as a live link to the official source. See `CLAUDE.md` under "Citations in
documents".

The `pravo-skills-cz` plugin has Czech-language workflows for contract review,
NDA triage and signature preparation. Its substantive content is **not** verified
law: treat it as a structure to fill, and check anything it asserts.
